Detailed Description

Numeric constants for the cutting-plane / bisection cores.

The initial radii are kept close to the coefficient scale: an ellipsoid much larger than the solution wastes iterations (the method needs \( O(n^2 \log(R/r)) \)), while one that is too small makes the subproblem infeasible. The MLE radius is shared by the CCP outer loop, which starts from the least-squares solution rather than the origin.
