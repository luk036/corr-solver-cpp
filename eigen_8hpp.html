double design_cond ( const std::vector< Arr > & Sigma)

2-norm condition number of the design matrix whose column k is vec(Sigma[k]). The m x m Gram matrix and its Jacobi eigen-decomposition are carried in double-double arithmetic: squaring the condition number pushes the smallest eigenvalue below double precision, so plain double loses ~1e-5 relative.
